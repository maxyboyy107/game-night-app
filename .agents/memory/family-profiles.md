---
name: Family profile identity
description: Product rules for account-managed family subprofiles and their game histories.
---

Family members are managed by the signed-in account and do not have separate logins. Associate game history with a stable family-member ID rather than the current name. Archive profiles instead of deleting them so past games remain accessible; archived profiles cannot join new games.

**Why:** Family players share one account, and renaming or archiving a profile must not detach or erase its past game records.

**How to apply:** Keep this ownership and history model consistent in future authentication, participant selection, profile, and game-history changes.
